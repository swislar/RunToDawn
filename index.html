Getting the file out of the Health app

  1. Open Health on iPhone and tap your profile picture, top right.
  2. Scroll down and tap Export All Health Data, then Export.
  3. The export takes a few minutes to build. Save it to Files, AirDrop it, or email it to yourself.
  4. You get export.zip. Bring it here as-is, or unzip it and use apple_health_export/export.xml.
On a big export, handing over the unzipped export.xml is faster and uses less memory than the zip.
